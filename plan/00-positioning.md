# Positioning

## Product Thesis

`silan.dev` is Silan Hu's public AI systems research workbench. It turns
papers, engineering work, and failed experiments into bounded claims, tested
findings, reusable artifacts, and evolving research questions.

The homepage says this in plain language:

> Silan Hu builds and studies AI systems. Here I share what I tested, what
> changed my mind, and what you can reuse.

The public promise is concrete:

> See what I am investigating, what I tested, what changed my mind, and what
> another researcher or engineer can reuse.

The internal metaphor may remain a "technical shelf", but that phrase must not
be the public value proposition. A shelf describes storage. The product exists
to expose reasoning, evidence, change, and reuse.

## Relationship To silan.tech

`silan.tech` records verified identity and completed outcomes. `silan.dev`
records how those outcomes were reasoned about, built, challenged, and revised.

| Question | `silan.tech` | `silan.dev` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is canonical here? | Identity, CV, publications, formal milestones, contact | Research questions, reading trails, experiments, technical decisions, failures, revisions |
| What does a project page prove? | The outcome and Silan's formal contribution | The decisions, evidence, tradeoffs, artifacts, and changing understanding behind the work |
| Why does a reader visit? | Verify who Silan is and what he has completed | Inspect how Silan thinks and reuse what he learned |
| Update trigger | A verified milestone changes | New evidence changes a question, claim, decision, or next test |
| Update cadence | Low and event-driven | Incremental, but quality-gated rather than quota-driven |
| Search role | Canonical person and outcome entity | Attributable topic evidence and research process |

The same source may appear on both sites only through an explicit projection.
One site owns the canonical full page; the other uses an excerpt or reference,
unless the two pages contain substantially different content and intent.

## Concrete Reader Jobs

### Researcher Or Senior Engineer

When evaluating an idea, find a claim, its evidence, its counterevidence, the
implementation artifact, and the conditions under which the conclusion holds.

### Collaborator, Technical Lead, Or Founder

Within three minutes, determine Silan's role, the decision he owned, the
alternatives considered, the result, the limitations, and the next unresolved
problem.

### Student Or Adjacent-Domain Reader

Enter through a plain-language problem statement, then follow a paper-to-test
or problem-to-artifact path without first understanding Silan Viking or the
site's taxonomy.

### Silan And His Future Self

Recover why a judgment was made, whether it was later supported or overturned,
what skill gap it exposed, and which next action remains open.

## Three-Minute Evidence Contract

- In 10 seconds, a visitor can state Silan's current technical focus and the
  difference between `silan.dev` and `silan.tech`.
- In 30 seconds, the homepage exposes three real evidence-backed entries:
  something being investigated, something recently tested, and something that
  changed a prior judgment.
- In 3 minutes, a reader can trace one project decision, one paper-to-practice
  transfer, and one failure or revision.
- Every featured entry exposes role, claim, evidence, outcome or current
  status, limits, and next test.

## Brand Evidence

The site must not describe Silan with unsupported adjectives. It should make
the following behaviors observable:

| Intended signal | Evidence shown by the site |
| --- | --- |
| Independent judgment | Disagreement, counterevidence, confidence, and changed-mind records |
| Research rigor | Source provenance, reproduction status, validity boundary, and revisit history |
| Engineering ability | Owned decisions, code or artifact, alternatives, measurements, and operational lessons |
| Intellectual curiosity | Connected questions that cross papers, systems, and experiments |
| Reliability | Explicit uncertainty, corrections, authorship role, and stale-content handling |
| Generosity | Reusable procedures, fixtures, diagrams, commands, or decision rules |

Personal charm should emerge from concrete curiosity, honest uncertainty,
precise disagreement, and visible progress. The design must not manufacture it
through slogans, decorative biography, or self-scoring.

Featured entries include one short, owner-written sentence answering either
"Why do I care about this now?" or "What surprised me?" This voice is not
generated from metadata and is never a substitute for evidence.

## Public Content Surfaces

1. **Current Questions**: a small set of active questions with latest evidence
   and next tests.
2. **Builds**: work Silan built or materially contributed to, with role and
   decision evidence.
3. **Reading Trails**: papers connected to critiques, reproductions,
   experiments, and project decisions.
4. **Field Notes**: engineering decisions, failures, corrections, and bounded
   lessons.
5. **Research Maps**: mature topic syntheses created only after enough
   qualifying evidence exists.
6. **Library**: items Silan studied or collected, visually and semantically
   separated from work he built.

## Non-Goals

- Do not mirror the `silan.tech` resume, publication list, or formal portfolio.
- Do not publish raw private notes or force every capture into a public post.
- Do not equate link collection, summaries, page count, stars, or posting
  frequency with insight or academic impact.
- Do not claim authority, ranking, citation, or model understanding without
  evidence.
- Do not make visitors understand Silan Viking before they can use the site.
- Do not add features that increase monthly site maintenance without reducing
  research friction or enabling a required reader job.

## Positioning Acceptance Gate

Before full frontend implementation:

- at least 4 of 5 test readers can explain the two-site difference after a
  10-second homepage exposure;
- at least 4 of 5 can identify one current question and one piece of evidence;
- no tester confuses collected work with work built or contributed by Silan;
- at least 3 of 5 technical readers can name a concrete artifact or decision
  they could reuse after a three-minute task.
